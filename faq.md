## Getting set up

**How do I start?**
Follow [Getting started](getting-started.md). It ends with a working setup rather than a tour.

**How long does setup take?**
For a first project on a small data source, one sitting. Large or unusual migrations take longer, so ask us before you plan around a date.

**Do I need a developer?**
> **Fill this in.** "No code required" is the single easiest claim to get wrong, and a reader who disproves it on day two remembers. Say which parts need engineering help and which do not.

## Accounts and access

**How do I add or remove someone?**
See [Invite your team](guides/invite-your-team.md). Removal lives in the same settings screen as invitations.

**Someone left the company. What do I do?**
Remove them from the project first, then rotate any credential they created. Closing a person's account does not retire the tokens they issued.

## Plans, data and limits

**What are the limits?**
Ask us and we will give you the numbers that apply to your plan. The shape of them is described in [What docs can do](features/overview.md).

**Can I export my data?**
Contact us and we will tell you exactly which formats are available today. We would rather answer precisely than promise a format that does not exist yet.

## When something goes wrong

**The product is behaving strangely. What should I send you?**
Three things, and they save a day of back-and-forth: what you expected, what happened instead, and the exact time it happened. A screenshot of the whole window beats a crop — the surrounding state is often the clue.

**Is there a status page?**
> **Fill this in:** link it here if you have one. If you do not, say how incidents are announced instead. Silence on this question reads as "nobody is watching".

## Adding to this page

The rule that keeps an FAQ useful: when a question arrives for the second time, write the answer here instead of answering it again. Keep answers under a hundred words — anything longer wants a page of its own.
